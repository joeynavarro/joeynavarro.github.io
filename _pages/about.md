I’m Joey Navarro. This is my personal academic and research website: a place for my ongoing research interests, analytical writing, and learning projects.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry and a master’s degree in Leadership and Change. I am pursuing doctoral study in Information Technology, with a research focus on the intention to implement artificial intelligence in healthcare.

## Research interests

My interests sit at the intersection of healthcare, information systems, and organizational behavior. I am interested in how people evaluate new technology, what they expect it to achieve, and what makes implementation workable in a real organization.

My current questions include:

- How do perceptions of AI’s usefulness relate to adoption intentions in healthcare?
- How do usability, complexity, training, and implementation effort shape those intentions?
- What roles do leadership, social influence, resources, and organizational conditions play?

These are the questions guiding my ongoing academic work. [Explore the research focus](/#research).

## Academic background and perspective

I am a Gates Millennium Scholar. I attended an engineering magnet high school before choosing to study biochemistry, broadening the way I approached scientific questions. My later study of Leadership and Change developed my interest in communication, self-leadership, organizational behavior, and how people learn from one another.

My healthcare and pharmacy technology experience adds a practical perspective to this academic work. It encourages me to consider workflows, the people using a system, and the setting in which an idea must operate.

I am also a tinkerer. Coding, electronics, hands-on projects, and learning new methods have long been part of how I explore a question. My technical background includes work with Python, R, Java, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Swift, and Scala.
